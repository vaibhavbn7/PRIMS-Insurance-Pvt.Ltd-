# Prism Insurance Analytics Dashboard
## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing insurance policy, premium, coverage, claim, and customer feedback data to help business stakeholders monitor insurance performance and improve decision-making. The dashboard was developed in Power BI using data stored in MS SQL Server, while Python was used for data cleaning and preprocessing. Additionally, customer feedback sentiment analysis was integrated to measure customer satisfaction and identify improvement areas.

## Problem Statement

Insurance companies generate large volumes of policy and claim data, making it difficult to monitor business performance and customer satisfaction efficiently. The objective of this project was to:

Track premium, coverage, and claim amounts across different policy types.
Monitor claim statuses such as Settled, Rejected, and Pending.
Analyze policy activity (Active vs Inactive).
Identify claim trends across customer age groups.
Measure customer sentiment from feedback data.
Provide a centralized dashboard for data-driven decision-making.

## Key Insights
## Business Performance
Total Premium Amount reached approximately 5.98M, indicating strong policy revenue generation.
Total Coverage Amount exceeded 600M, reflecting a large insured customer base.
Total Claim Amount was approximately 16.91M, helping assess risk exposure and claim liabilities.
## Policy Analysis
Travel Insurance generated the highest premium contribution (~2.5M).
Home Insurance contributed the lowest premium amount among all policy types.
More than 55% of policies were active, indicating healthy customer retention.
## Claims Analysis
Rejected claims represented the highest claim status volume (~4.4K), highlighting opportunities to improve claim processing transparency.
Pending claims remained significant, suggesting potential process optimization opportunities.
Adults generated the highest claim amount, while Young Adults contributed the lowest claim value.
## Customer Insights
Sentiment Analysis revealed customer satisfaction trends and enabled identification of positive, neutral, and negative feedback patterns.
Combining sentiment data with claim and policy metrics provided a holistic view of customer experience and business performance.
## Business Impact
Improved visibility into insurance operations through a centralized reporting solution.
Enabled faster identification of high-performing policy categories and claim trends.
Supported data-driven decisions for policy management, claims processing, and customer experience improvement.
Reduced manual reporting efforts through automated Power BI dashboards.
